## 1. Project Objective

Build a driver-assistance system for a small electric car that, using a **single 2D LiDAR as the primary sensor**, keeps a
human-driven car from colliding while intervening **only when the driver would not have avoided the collision themselves**, and
demonstrates this with measurable evidence (digital twin, Monte Carlo, real-car logs).

## 2. Problem Statement

Production ADAS relies on many sensors (cameras, radars, ultrasonics, LiDAR) and large compute. Simple collision-avoidance systems
on small platforms either stop for everything (many needless interventions, the driver fights the system) or react too late.
The project must show that one LiDAR plus a model of the **driver's intent** is enough to:

1. predict a collision early enough to prevent it at the car's real braking and steering limits;
2. tell a driver who is about to avoid the obstacle from one who is not, and hold back needless takeovers;
3. choose the least intrusive action (warning -> speed limit -> steering correction -> evasive manoeuvre -> brake);
4. be verified without risking hardware, on a digital twin that runs the car's own code.

## 3. System Goals

| ID | Goal | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| G-1 | Zero collisions caused by driver error inside the operating envelope (§ 3.1) | Monte Carlo + scenario suite + real-car tests |
| G-2 | Fewer needless interventions than a plain (intent-unaware) ADAS, with statistical significance | Paired Monte Carlo, counterfactual ground truth |
| G-3 | No loss of mobility: the assisted car reaches as many goals, as fast, as plain ADAS | Monte Carlo goals / time-to-goal |
| G-4 | Graceful degradation on sensor, link or compute faults | Fault-injection scenarios, health monitor |
| G-5 | A driver-facing HMI of production quality (EV cockpit conventions) | GUI review, smoke tests |
| G-6 | Reproducible evidence: every result regenerated by a script in the repository | Test suite, evaluation scripts |

### 3.1 Operating envelope (ODD)

| Parameter | Range |
|---|---|
| Environment | Indoor, flat floor, static walls and furniture, pedestrians / other RC cars moving ≤ 2 m/s |
| Speed | 0 - 0.9 m/s (≈ 0 - 45 km/h full-size equivalent at 1:14) |
| Lighting | Any (LiDAR primary); the camera functions require normal indoor light |
| Obstacles | Objects taller than the LiDAR plane (~12 cm) and wider than 2 cm; not glass / mirrors, not black light-absorbing surfaces |
| Driver | Human with a gamepad / keyboard over WiFi, may be attentive, late, aggressive or momentarily inattentive |
| Out of scope | Outdoors, slopes, wet floors (warning only), objects below the scan plane, multi-floor maps |

## 4. Functional Requirements

Priority: **Critical** = safety or core claim; **High** = required for the project result; **Medium** = valuable, not blocking.
Verification: **T** test (automated), **S** simulation (twin / Monte Carlo), **C** real-car test, **I** inspection / review.
Status: **Met** (verified), **Met-S** (verified in the twin, not yet on the car), **Partial**, **Open**.

### 4.1 Perception

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria | Ver. | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | **LiDAR obstacle detection.** The system shall convert every LiDAR scan into obstacle points in the vehicle frame, correcting the sensor's mounting offset and yaw. | Critical | Points of a known object placed 0.3-3 m ahead reported within ±3 cm range and ±2° bearing; scan processed within one scan period. | T, C | Met |
| FR-002 | **Blind-zone memory.** Obstacles that leave the LiDAR's minimum range (near the body) shall be remembered and moved with the car's motion until they are passed. | Critical | An obstacle approached to 5 cm is still braked for; no phantom obstacle persists after it was passed (memory pruned by distance / age). | T, S | Met |
| FR-003 | **Free-space detection.** The system shall compute the free distance along the car's predicted swept path (body width + margin) at the current and at alternative steering angles, forward and reverse. | Critical | Free distance to a wall at 0.5-3 m within ±3 cm; a gap narrower than the body reported as blocked; a gap ≥ body + 2×3 cm reported passable. | T, S | Met |
| FR-004 | **Object tracking.** Clusters shall be tracked across scans with position and velocity; moving objects (0.15-2 m/s, ≥ 5 consistent scans) shall be flagged. | High | Crossing person tracked with speed error < 0.1 m/s; static objects never flagged as moving under 20 mm range noise. | T, S | Met-S |
| FR-005 | **Ego-motion estimation.** Speed, yaw rate and world pose shall be estimated from LiDAR scan matching fused with the throttle model. | Critical | Speed RMSE ≤ 5 cm/s, yaw-rate RMSE ≤ 3°/s on twin and on real logs; pose drift ≤ 2 % of distance. | T, S, C | Met |
| FR-006 | **Loop closure / map.** The system shall build a map from submaps and correct accumulated pose drift when it revisits a place. | Medium | End-of-loop position error reduced by ≥ 80 % on a drifting loop; return-to-start ends ≤ 30 cm from the true start. | T, S | Met-S |
| FR-007 | **Sensor latency awareness.** The system shall measure the scan latency online and widen its stopping margin accordingly. | High | No contact at full throttle with up to 0.3 s extra scan latency. | S | Met-S |
| FR-008 | **Rear camera (optional).** With a rear camera fitted, the system shall show reversing guidelines, a predicted "ghost" footprint, objects and floor patches in the reversing path. | Medium | Guidelines follow the steering; contact distance to an object in the path within ±5 cm; works without the camera (graceful empty state). | T | Met-S |

### 4.2 Prediction and decision

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria | Ver. | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-010 | **Collision prediction.** The system shall predict, every control cycle, whether the car will contact an obstacle along its swept path within its stopping distance (reaction + braking at the measured deceleration). | Critical | 100 % of the scenario suite's collision courses detected in time to stop; stops within 2-8 cm of a wall from full speed. | T, S, C | Met |
| FR-011 | **Driver-intent estimation (ML inference).** A learned model shall estimate P(the driver, left alone, contacts or nearly contacts something within 2 s) from the stick, throttle, speed and free-space history. | Critical | Held-out AP ≥ 0.80, AUC ≥ 0.90, calibration error ≤ 0.03 on the twin; ≥ 0.80 AP on unseen randomised cars; inference ≤ 2 ms on the Pi. | T, S | Met-S |
| FR-012 | **Physics floor.** The learned risk shall never be lower than a physics-based floor (driver inactive while the free way is shorter than the stopping distance). | Critical | Unit test: floor = 0.95 in that state regardless of the model output. | T | Met |
| FR-013 | **Intent may only withhold, never cause, an intervention.** The learned model shall be able to hold back a steering takeover but shall never suppress the brake. | Critical | Brake decisions identical with and without the model in every scenario. | T, S | Met |
| FR-014 | **Stuck / no-progress detection.** When the car makes no progress for ≥ 5 s with the throttle held, trust in the driver shall be withdrawn and the evasive manoeuvre allowed. | High | No drive in the Monte Carlo ends stuck where plain ADAS reached the goal (current: 2/72 fail for both systems). | S | Partial |
| FR-015 | **Formal safe distance.** The RSS longitudinal safe distance shall be computed and shown. | Medium | Matches the published formula (unit test); shown in the HMI while moving. | T | Met |
| FR-016 | **Online adaptation.** The displayed risk shall recalibrate to the current driver from observable safety events. | Medium | Calibration error reduced ≥ 20 % prequentially on held-out drives; takeover decision unaffected. | T, S | Met-S |

### 4.3 Driver warning and intervention

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria | Ver. | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-020 | **Staged warning logic.** The system shall escalate: forward-collision warning (≤ 1.6 s to contact) -> speed limit -> brake, shown in the HMI. | Critical | Each stage shown before the next acts; warning precedes a needed brake by ≥ 0.5 s in ≥ 80 % of twin drives. | T, S | Met-S |
| FR-021 | **Emergency braking (path gate).** The throttle shall be cut or reversed when the free distance falls below the stopping distance; the hold is released only when the driver lets go or the path clears. | Critical | 0 contacts in the scenario suite and Monte Carlo; release rule unit-tested. | T, S, C | Met |
| FR-022 | **Steering correction (nudge).** A small steering change shall be preferred to braking when it makes the path safe. | Medium | Passes a box that would clip the body by 3 cm without braking (nominal twin). | S | Partial (55 % on randomised cars) |
| FR-023 | **Evasive steering.** When a collision is imminent and a path around exists, the car shall steer around it (Hybrid A*, MPPI fallback) and return control to the driver. | High | Evasive scenarios pass; no contact; control handed back when the driver steers against it or releases the throttle. | T, S | Met-S |
| FR-024 | **Moving obstacles.** For a crossing or oncoming object the system shall choose pass / yield / stop / move away. | High | No contact in the moving-obstacle scenarios; no needless wait for a late crosser. | S | Met-S |
| FR-025 | **Driver override.** Steering against a manoeuvre or releasing the throttle shall hand control back; an explicit override mode shall disable all assists. | Critical | Hand-back within 0.35 s of sustained counter-steer / 0.9 s of released throttle; override state shown in red. | T | Met |
| FR-026 | **Speed-limit zones and drive modes.** User-drawn zones (km/h, scaled 1:14) and Eco / Normal / Sport shall cap the throttle; safety margins are identical in all modes. | Medium | Throttle capped inside a zone and 0.5 s before entering; Eco cap 55 %. | T | Met |
| FR-027 | **Realistic steering envelope.** Maximum steering shall decrease with speed like a full-size car (≈ 0.6 g lateral at 1:14). | High | Lateral acceleration at full lock and full throttle ≤ 1.2 m/s². | S | Met |

### 4.5 HMI, data and validation

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria | Ver. | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-040 | **GUI visualisation.** A desktop HMI shall show speed (km/h scaled), gear, steering range, driver risk, safety status, 3D scene with obstacles coloured by relevance and the predicted path, mini-map; secondary functions in drawers. | High | All drawers fit at 1100×700 … 2560×1440; dark and light themes; no-data states designed; smoke tests pass. | T, I | Met |
| FR-041 | **Live link.** The HMI shall update from the relay at ≥ 20 Hz and indicate a lost link within 2.5 s. | High | Measured update rate; NO DATA shown after 2.5 s without packets, not before. | T | Met |
| FR-042 | **Data recording.** Every drive shall be logged: scans, driver commands, commands sent, assist decisions, health. | Critical | A recorded drive can be replayed through current code with identical inputs (log replay tool). | T, C | Met |
| FR-043 | **Log replay.** Recorded real drives shall be replayable through the current code to compare decisions. | High | Replay reproduces the logged scans and commands; decision counts reported. | T | Met |
| FR-044 | **Digital twin.** A simulator shall run the car's own relay code against a simulated LiDAR and vehicle with fitted dynamics and domain randomisation. | Critical | Twin path error vs real log within 5 cm over a logged drive; randomisation of ≥ 10 parameters. | T, S | Met |
| FR-045 | **Monte Carlo validation.** Driver-only, brake-only, ADAS and ADAS + intent shall drive identical randomised drives; needless interventions judged by a counterfactual (same driver, left alone, 2 s). | Critical | Paired statistics reported (Wilcoxon); results visible in the Monte Carlo lab with cars replayed in 3D. | T, S | Met |
| FR-046 | **Fault injection.** LiDAR dropout, frozen and late scans, command loss and spurious points shall be simulated. | High | 0 contacts in all fault scenarios. | S | Met-S |
| FR-047 | **ML training pipeline.** Twin data generation, GPU training, held-out validation and export to a numpy model for the Pi shall be reproducible from scripts and visible in the training lab. | High | Exported model gives identical outputs in numpy and in the training framework. | T | Met |
| FR-048 | **Calibration tools** for LiDAR offset, servo centre, speed / stopping and turning, run only on request, results applied only after confirmation. | High | Each calibration writes a result file; nothing changes until "Apply". | C, I | Met |

## 10. Constraints and Assumptions

1. Calibrations are run only when the operator asks; the system uses the last applied calibration.
2. The car is only driven with an operator holding the throttle (dead-man switch) in every autonomous mode.
3. Needless / needed is defined by the counterfactual: the same driver, left alone for 2 s, comes within 2 cm of an obstacle or not.
4. The twin's driver models (lapsing, late, good, distracted, aggressive) stand in for human drivers until real-driver data is collected.
5. Results marked Met-S are verified in the digital twin only; they become Met after a real-car test.
