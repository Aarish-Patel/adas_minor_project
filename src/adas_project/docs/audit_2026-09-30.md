# ADAS production-hardening audit - 30 Sep 2026

Scope: the master brief of 30 Sep (click-to-go, re-planning, evasive steering, speed / zone limits, parallel and perpendicular parking,
obstacle memory, localisation, dashboard, Raspberry Pi performance) plus the user's correction that parallel parking has no predefined
spaces. Everything below was measured in the project's own twin and HMI; nothing has been run on the car yet. Commits f16a9fc .. 98c28e2.

## 1. Architecture changes

| Area | Before | Now | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parallel parking | fixed spaces S0..S7 with occupied flags; Hybrid A* multi-point (9 s, failed below 1.1 m) | one continuous lane; gaps found from the LiDAR; the viable slot nearest to the click; reverse S, or a multi-stroke exit plan driven backwards for tight gaps (~10 ms); the gap measured again beside it; stall -> re-plan from where the car stands | ADR-032, ADR-033 |
| Bay parking | ArUco marker only | marker = which bay; LiDAR = the wall's line and the final stop | ADR-036 |
| Localisation | scan-to-scan ICP (3-5 % along a street) | scan-to-local-map, point-to-line, degeneracy-aware (`LocalMapOdometry`); obstacle memory re-anchored on it | ADR-034 |
| Click-to-go | re-plans on blockage / stall | + goal on an obstacle -> as near as the car fits; steer before moving at a standstill | TODO AB3, AB7 |
| Twin | wall-clock budgets, measured delay, SLAM thread: runs not repeatable | node budgets, work-based Pi-time model (fitted), inline SLAM: identical results on repeat | ADR-035 |
| HMI | free 16 ms timer; intermittent crashes | frames paced by the buffer swap; worker-thread signals via bridges; garbage only on the GUI thread; `--measure` profiling | ADR-037 |

## 4. Remaining known limitations
- Parking accuracy after long approaches: the parking suite passes 64-71 of 86 at the strict 5 cm / 5 deg target depending on the
  planning-time model (0 contacts, 0 stuck; median 3.2 cm, p90 8.8 cm, max 12.1 cm). The misses are 5-12 cm after 5-10 m approaches
  (odometry along a street).
- The kerb is invisible to a 2D LiDAR: the lane's lateral position comes from the car's pose.
- Forward parallel parking into a dead end is refused (the long front end swings into the kerb with this steering).
- Real UDP path: the lane-end case chose the neighbouring gap in 1 run of ~6 (4/4 correct on repeat).
- Needless evasive takeovers in the Monte Carlo are higher than the old baseline (adas+intent 34 -> 44 per 120 drives): the old
  number was measured with plan times inflated by CPU contention; it is an arbitration matter for the decider (TODO AB12).
- Nothing measured on the Pi or the car yet: Pi numbers are laptop x 3.5; odometry, parking and camera must be confirmed on the car.

## 6. FPS, CPU, memory
- HMI (laptop 1707 x 1067 @ 165 Hz, twin relay driving): 92 FPS bare / 64 FPS with the map drawer; 375 MB RSS; ~1.1 laptop cores.
- Relay (twin on the laptop): 120 MB RSS; 25 % of a laptop core; health NORMAL (LiDAR 10 Hz, link 20 Hz) with the HMI running.
- Control loop (`sim.profile_relay`, 30 drives): 4.2 ms per 50 ms tick on the laptop = ~14.7 ms on the Pi 5 (budget 20 ms); planner in
  its worker process median 23 ms / p95 355 ms (laptop).

## 7. Confidence for the car test
- High: braking and the no-contact behaviour (unchanged gate, 0 crashes in every suite), click-to-go and re-planning, the HMI.
- Medium: parallel parking on short approaches (<= 3 m) and bay parking - both depend on the odometry and the rear camera behaving as in
  the twin.
- To measure first on the car: a 5 m straight drive against a taped mark (odometry), the control-loop timing
